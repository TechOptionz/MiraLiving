Bargara

Queensland’s best-kept coastal secret

Just 20 minutes from Bundaberg, Bargara blends natural beauty with village charm. Start your day strolling the Esplanade or sipping coffee on Bauer Street, then spend the afternoon on the fairways of Bargara Golf Club. With every convenience close by and the ocean at your door, Bargara feels a world away — yet right where you need to be.

01 · Shopping

Bargara Central

Your grocer, pharmacy and specialty stores, plus Bargara Meats, the local butcher known for hand-made sausages. For fresh produce and gelato, Bargara Berries on Hughes Road is a favourite.

02 · Cafés & dining

Bauer Street to the sea

Start the day at the award-winning Windmill Café, wander to Rick’s at Bargara for brunch with ocean views, book a sunset table at Kacy’s, then wind down at Tipsea with cocktails and share plates.

03 · Recreation

Beaches & fairways

Morning walks along the foreshore lead to Kellys Beach and the sheltered waters of The Basin. Bargara Golf Club, an 18-hole course framed by the coast, is three minutes’ walk from the door.

04 · Healthcare

Care close at hand

Local GP care at Bargara Medical Centre and Pharmacy Central. Friendly Society Private, Mater Private and Bundaberg Base Hospital are 15–20 minutes away, with the new $1.2 billion Bundaberg Hospital under way.

Neighbouring · 3 min walk

Bargara Golf Club

A refined 18-hole course framed by coastal landscapes, with practice facilities and social rounds at sunset. Undulating fairways, sculpted greens and seaside breezes.

Regional investment · Under construction

New Bundaberg Hospital

A $1.2 billion investment under Queensland’s Big Build programme: additional beds, expanded emergency and diagnostic facilities, and teaching capacity for the region.

The Collaborators

The people behind Mira

Two decades of residential craft, an architecture practice that builds what it draws, a local builder who knows this coastline, and an interior designer with an instinct for how a home should feel. Four disciplines, one address. Ground was turned in January 2025 and the building was completed in September 2026.

Developer

Furtado Property

Over 20 years of residential property across South-East Queensland, with a philosophy of creating buildings that endure: quality workmanship combined with thoughtful, timeless design. At Mira Living that commitment delivers a landmark of refined coastal living with lasting value.

Architect

Sparc

Sparc’s reputation for residential and multi-residential projects brings together pragmatic vision, constructional clarity and innovation, for architecture that embodies prestige, practicality and a profound sense of place.

Builder

IDC Construct

The boundary-pushing local builder bringing precision and innovation to projects across Wide Bay, Queensland. Every pour, every bar tied and every truss lifted at Mira was done by crews from the region.

Interior Designer

Sarah Wood Designs

Over 15 years across fashion, interiors and construction, with an instinctive eye for colour, texture and detail. Interiors that feel both contemporary and timeless, translated into a cohesive, personal expression of home.
